### What we care about

- **Clarity in everyday work.** Give each task, record and revision a clear place.
- **People in control.** Keep review and decisions visible as work moves forward.
- **A shared standard.** Design for accessibility, accountability and thoughtful handling of project information.

### A family taking shape

StruktaOne, StruktaFlow and StruktaLog are in development. Product access and release details will be announced here as they become available.

StruktaOne and StruktaLog focus on construction. StruktaFlow supports workflows across industries. Each product has its own scope; cross-product integrations will be described as they become available.
